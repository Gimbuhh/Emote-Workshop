## Added

- **Emoji size** offers 128×128 and 256×256 Discord emoji exports, so you can try a larger image without changing the framing. The selection applies to PNGs, animated GIFs, previews, and ZIP downloads, with both sizes kept under 256 KB. Discord may resize uploaded emoji.

## Changed

- **Compare** opens the exported image at **Actual size**; turning that off enlarges it for inspection. Both images show their pixel dimensions, and the headings stay aligned with the previews. Guidance below the editing canvas points to the actual export and chat previews when checking detail.

## Fixed

- Static Discord and Twitch PNG exports show their format without the misleading **AVIF unavailable** warning.
